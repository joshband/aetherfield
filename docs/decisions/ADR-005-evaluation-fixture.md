# ADR-005 — S2 evaluation fixture: FDN line count, the concrete delay-length set, and the fixture's sample rates

## Review summary

- **Decision:** fix S2's FDN line-count bracket, delay-length set, and fixture sample rates.
- **Why:** deterministic validation requires concrete, reproducible inputs.
- **Consequence:** the S2 fixture is implemented and verified with those constraints.

## ADR-005 — S2 evaluation fixture: FDN line count, the concrete delay-length set, and the fixture's sample rates

**Status:** Accepted

This ADR decides three quantities and nothing else: the line count `N` **of the S2 evaluation fixture**, the concrete delay-length set `mᵢ` for that fixture, and the sample rate(s) the fixture targets. It decides no topology, no matrix family, no damping filter form, no denormal mechanism, no parameter semantics, no `T60` endpoint values, no `D_max`, no diffusion, no stereo strategy, no interpolation, and **no modulation**. It authorizes no implementation and no source file. Every quantitative statement below is arithmetic on stated assumptions or a literature citation — **never a measurement**, because Aetherfield has still measured nothing about reverb (testing.md). The four computations in Evidence were performed while drafting this ADR; they are arithmetic over the values decided here, not observations of any Aetherfield code, of which none relevant exists.

**No sonic claim is made anywhere in this ADR.** A line count, a modal-density figure and an echo-count bound are combinatorial and spectral quantities. They do not establish that the S2 fixture will sound smooth, spacious or uncoloured; §20's "it seems stable" prohibition and ADR-002's symmetric warning — "the math is clean, so it will sound good" is the same error — apply unchanged. The Rationale's closing subsection states plainly what this fixture is predicted *not* to demonstrate.

### Context

ADR-002 selected the topology and then deferred exactly two numbers out of it:

> **Line count.** `N = 8` is the baseline to be evaluated, bracketed by `N = 4` (cost floor) and `N = 16` (density ceiling). The final N is **DEFERRED** to measured evidence and will be recorded in a follow-up ADR. This ADR fixes the structure, not the number.
>
> **Delay lengths.** Mutually co-prime, chosen from a time-domain specification rather than a sample count. Specific values are **DEFERRED**.

ADR-003 restated both deferrals verbatim ("Still DEFERRED, unchanged: `N`, the delay lengths `mᵢ`"), and ADR-004 restated them again and made two of its own quantities depend on them: `T60_max` is *derived* from `m_min`, `T60_min` from `m_max`. docs/phases/phase1-s2-verification-plan.md then recorded the consequence in its "Dependencies not yet decided" section: **all twenty verification cases NS-1…NS-11 and PT-1…PT-9 are blocked on a decided `N` and a decided `mᵢ`** — "until that follow-up ADR is accepted, none of these cases can be run against real numbers."

**The deadlock this ADR exists to break.** ADR-002 deferred the final `N` *to measured evidence*. The only source of such evidence is the S2 network. The S2 network cannot be built, and therefore cannot be measured, without an `N` and a delay set. Taken literally, ADR-002's deferral is unsatisfiable: it conditions a decision on evidence whose production the same decision gates. This is not a defect in ADR-002's reasoning about topology; it is a consequence of ADR-002 using one symbol, `N`, for two different quantities — the number the *experiment* is run at, and the number the *product* ships. Resolving the deadlock honestly means separating them, not inventing a measurement.

**A second, narrower gap blocks the same cases.** Five of the twenty bounds — NS-5, NS-6, NS-7, PT-4, PT-9 — are written "at every supported sample rate," and PT-9's entire subject is invariance *under* a rate change. **No document in this repository enumerates a supported sample-rate list.** Phase 0's renderer and S1's tests use 48 kHz (testing.md, README.md), but that is a fixture choice, not a product commitment. With one rate the five clauses are vacuous and the specific hazard they exist to catch — ADR-002's "a coefficient cached across a rate change silently changes RT60," and ADR-003 (d)'s requirement that `mᵢ`, `γ₀`, `γ_π`, `gᵢ` and `aᵢ` all be re-derived — is untestable. This ADR closes only as much of that gap as (b) requires and says so; the product's supported-rate matrix is a wider question it deliberately does not answer.

**Inherited constraints this ADR operates under, restated rather than assumed remembered.** ADR-002 fixed the FDN topology, the single real orthogonal feedback matrix with a normalized Hadamard baseline and a Householder fallback, in-loop per-line placement of `gᵢ` and `Hᵢ(z)`, and homogeneous Jot decay `gᵢ = γ^mᵢ`. ADR-003 fixed the one-pole damping filter and its derivation, `a_max = 0.999`, the deterministic denormal cutoff `ε = 1e-20`, control-boundary validation including "`mᵢ ≥ 1`, mutually distinct, `≤` reserved capacity," and the `prepare`/`reset`/rate-change contract in which `prepare` takes `N` and a *time-domain* delay specification and never delay lengths in samples. ADR-004 fixed the three-control surface, the ramp and the transport. **None of that is reopened, weakened or re-argued here.**

**The modulation prohibition is restated in full, as ADR-003 (e) and ADR-004 (d) each restated it.** ADR-002 "authorizes no modulation of any kind"; its stability proof is for a **time-invariant** network and does not survive time-varying delay lengths, because a time-varying delay resamples what it stores and the delay bank stops being norm-preserving. ADR-003 (e) defines the only two admissible future routes — Path A (unitary feedback-matrix modulation, bars A1–A4) and Path B (empirical, bars B1–B5) — and neither bar is met. **Deciding `mᵢ` does not move modulation one step closer to authorization.** The delay lengths decided below are *constants of a preparation*, re-derived only on a re-`prepare` (ADR-003 (d)), never swept, interpolated or modulated at render time. ADR-004 already recorded why the accepted control surface is structurally incapable of reaching them; nothing here changes that.

Sol decides; Astra accepts; Terra implements when separately authorized; Luna verifies.

### Decision

#### (a) Line count: the S2 evaluation fixture is `N = 8`; the product's `N` remains DEFERRED

**Select (a1).** ADR-002's single symbol `N` is separated into two quantities, and only the first is decided here:

- **`N_fixture = 8`.** The S2 evaluation fixture — the network that NS-1…NS-11 and PT-1…PT-9 are executed against — has **eight** delay lines. This is **DECIDED**, on the same literature-and-arithmetic grounds ADR-002 already used to name 8 as its baseline, sharpened in the Rationale. **It is not a measured result and must never be described as one.** It is the configuration that exists in order to *produce* the measured evidence ADR-002 asked for.
- **`N_product` remains DEFERRED**, to exactly the evidence ADR-002 named: S2's NS/PT results plus testing.md's Sonic acceptance gate. A further ADR records it. This ADR is the follow-up ADR-002 anticipated, and it discharges half of it: ADR-002 conditioned the final `N` on evidence that its own deferral prevented anyone from generating, and the honest repair is to build the experiment now and keep the product number open, not to relabel a literature argument as a measurement.

**ADR-002's bracket becomes an executable comparison, not a remark.** `N = 4` and `N = 16` are **required runnable configurations** of the same fixture, over the same evidence list, at the same delay-time endpoints fixed in (b). This costs no new mechanism: ADR-003 (d) already specifies that `prepare` takes `N` and a time-domain delay specification, so `N` is a preparation-time quantity by an accepted decision, not a compile-time constant. Because (b)'s rule places `N` points on a **fixed time interval**, moving from 4 to 8 to 16 changes the modal density and the mixing while holding the network's shortest line, longest line and time span constant — the bracket therefore varies the quantity ADR-002 said it should, with the others controlled.

**`N` is restricted to powers of two, for a reason inherited from ADR-002, not chosen here.** A Hadamard matrix can exist only at orders 1, 2 and positive multiples of 4, and the Sylvester construction — the one whose fast transform gives ADR-002's `N log₂N` addition count — produces orders `2^k`. At `N = 12` a Hadamard matrix exists by other constructions but carries no fast transform, so ADR-002's stated matrix cost would not hold. `{4, 8, 16}` is therefore not an arbitrary bracket: it is precisely the set of Sylvester–Hadamard orders in the plausible range, and ADR-002's matrix decision is what restricts it.

#### (b) Delay lengths: `N` geometrically spaced times on `[27 ms, 81 ms]`, each snapped to the nearest prime sample count

**Select (b1).** The delay specification is **a pair of times and a derivation rule**, exactly as ADR-002 required; sample counts are outputs of `prepare`, never inputs to it.

> **Time-domain specification (the stored quantity).** `t_min = 27 ms`, `t_max = 81 ms`, so `t_max/t_min = 3` exactly.
>
> **Target times.** `tᵢ = t_min · (t_max/t_min)^(i/(N−1))`, for `i = 0 … N−1` — `N` points geometrically (log-uniformly) spaced, inclusive of both endpoints.
>
> **Sample counts.** `mᵢ = ` the prime nearest to `round(f_s · tᵢ)`. **Tie-break:** on an exact tie, take the smaller prime. **Totality guard:** process `i` in increasing order and require `mᵢ > mᵢ₋₁`; if the nearest prime is not, take the smallest prime greater than `mᵢ₋₁`. The guard is **inert at every configuration computed in Evidence** — it exists so the rule is total at any rate, not because it fires here.

At `N = 8`, this yields the following, which are the numbers a test fixture can use directly:

- **`N = 4` @ 48 kHz:** `1297, 1867, 2693, 3889` (Σ = 9746); **@ 44.1 kHz:** `1193, 1721, 2477, 3571` (Σ = 8962).
- **`N = 16` @ 48 kHz:** `1297, 1399, 1499, 1613, 1733, 1867, 2011, 2161, 2333, 2503, 2693, 2903, 3121, 3359, 3613, 3889` (Σ = 37994); **@ 44.1 kHz:** `1193, 1279, 1381, 1483, 1597, 1721, 1847, 1987, 2137, 2297, 2477, 2663, 2861, 3083, 3319, 3571` (Σ = 34896).

**Properties that are now assertable at `prepare`, and must be asserted rather than trusted:** every `mᵢ` is prime; all `mᵢ` are strictly increasing and therefore distinct; every pair is co-prime (`gcd(mᵢ, mⱼ) = 1` for `i ≠ j`), which is ADR-002's requirement and is checked, not inferred from primality, because the check is `N(N−1)/2 ≤ 120` gcds at preparation time. This satisfies ADR-003 (c)'s existing rule "`mᵢ ≥ 1`, mutually distinct, `≤` reserved capacity" without adding a new gate; a violation **fails `prepare`** under ADR-003 (d) rule 5 and changes nothing (rule 7).

**Reserved capacity.** `C = round(f_s_max · t_max) + 64` samples per line, where `f_s_max` is the highest rate the preparation supports. At the fixture's 48 kHz this is `C = 3952` floats per line — `123.5 KiB` at `N = 8`, `61.8 KiB` at `N = 4`, `247.0 KiB` at `N = 16`. The `+64` is the prime-search headroom: the largest prime gap below 5000 is 34, so a ±64 window around any target in this range is guaranteed to contain a prime, and `m_max ≤ C` is therefore satisfiable and must still be asserted. At 44.1 kHz the selected `m_max = 3571 ≤ 3952`, so reserving at 48 kHz covers both fixture rates, as ADR-003 (d) rule 2 requires.

**What this ADR does *not* decide about storage, deliberately.** Whether the S2 network gives every line the uniform capacity `C` with a separate read offset, or prepares each line at exactly `mᵢ`, is an **interface question belonging to Terra's separate S2 task plan**, not to this ADR. Both are expressible through S1's existing primitive: `DelayLine::prepare(double sampleRate, std::size_t maxDelaySamples)` yields `y[n] = x[n − maxDelaySamples]`, so `line[i].prepare(f_s, mᵢ)` realizes line `i` directly. The two options differ only in spare capacity, and ADR-003 (d) rule 2's *purpose* — spare room for read-offset-based Size and pre-delay, so neither needs a reallocation — is currently moot, because **ADR-004 defers both Size and Pre-delay**. The arithmetic for both options is recorded above so that whichever Terra chooses is checkable; a later Size or pre-delay decision reopens the capacity rule, and is named in Revisit When.

**Still DEFERRED, unchanged and not touched by anything above:** `T60_min`, `T60_max`, `D_max`, `δ_margin`'s concrete value above its `1e-3` floor, the input injection vector, the output tap design, stereo extraction, the diffusion stages, interpolation, and modulation.

#### (c) The S2 fixture targets 48 kHz and 44.1 kHz — and that is a fixture scope, not a product commitment

**Select (c2).** The S2 evaluation fixture is prepared and executed at **48 000 Hz** (primary — the existing precedent from Phase 0's renderer and S1's tests, and the rate at which the delay set above is tabulated) and at **44 100 Hz** (secondary). For the twenty S2 cases, and for those cases only, the phrase "at every supported sample rate" in NS-5, NS-6, NS-7, PT-4 and PT-9 resolves to **"at each of these two rates."** ADR-003 (d) rule 2's "highest supported sample rate" evaluates, for this fixture, to 48 kHz.

44.1 kHz is included for one stated reason and no other: **two rates is the minimum at which rate-dependence is observable at all**, and `48000/44100 = 160/147` is not an integer ratio, so an implementation that scaled coefficients or delay lengths instead of re-deriving them from time — precisely ADR-002's named "a coefficient cached across a rate change silently changes RT60" hazard, widened by ADR-003 to `aᵢ` — cannot pass by accident. PT-9's entire subject is invariance under a rate change and is unexecutable at a single rate.

> **Explicitly out of scope, and explicitly still open.** This ADR does **not** decide which sample rates Aetherfield supports. It does not decide whether 88.2, 96, 176.4 or 192 kHz are supported, what happens at a rate outside the supported set beyond ADR-003 (c)'s existing "`prepare` fails; prior preparation untouched," what the product's reserved-capacity ceiling is, or what a host may negotiate. **No document in this repository enumerates a supported-rate list, and none does after this ADR.** That is a real and wider gap; closing it is a product and integration question, entangled with ADR-001's deferred AUv3 framework decision, and deciding it inside a fixture ADR would be exactly the mandate creep this ADR's other two sections are written to avoid. It is named in Revisit When as an open item, not resolved.

This ADR decides `N_fixture`, `mᵢ` and the fixture's rates. It does **not** decide or imply: the topology, the matrix family, the damping filter form, the decay law, the denormal mechanism, the reset/lifecycle contract, parameter names/units/ranges/mappings/smoothing/transport, `T60_min`/`T60_max`/`D_max`, input or output diffusion, stereo decorrelation or tap design, delay-line interpolation, Freeze/Infinite, Bloom, Texture, pitch or spectral processing, multi-engine architecture, the AUv3 framework, or the product's supported sample rates. **And it authorizes no modulation of any kind**: ADR-002's prohibition and ADR-003 (e)'s Path A (A1–A4) and Path B (B1–B5) stand exactly as written, this ADR opens no third path and relaxes no bar, and fixing `mᵢ` as preparation-time constants is the opposite of making them time-varying.

### Rationale

#### Why the fixture/product split is the only honest resolution

ADR-002's deferral is a good instinct expressed as an unsatisfiable condition. Its instinct is right: a line count is a perceptual and cost trade that literature cannot settle for a specific product, and ADR-002 was correct to refuse to settle it. But "deferred to measured evidence" only works when someone else can produce the measurement, and here nobody can, because the measurement requires the very fixture the deferral blocks. The three escapes are to fabricate the evidence (excluded by §20 and by every ADR's own labelling discipline), to stay blocked forever (which abandons Phase 1's successor milestone), or to notice that `N` is doing two jobs. The third is correct, and it is not a loophole: after this ADR the product's `N` is still deferred to S2's measured evidence, and S2 can now produce it. **The bracket is what makes this more than a relabelling.** If `N = 4` and `N = 16` were not required runnable configurations, "fixture baseline" would be an evasion; because they are, S2 produces a three-point comparison over the exact bracket ADR-002 specified, which is the evidence a successor ADR needs.

#### Why 8, and not 4 or 16, for the fixture

ADR-002 made this argument and this ADR does not re-derive it; three points are added because the concrete delay set makes them checkable.

First, **mixing uniformity ties the line count to the matrix decision.** ADR-002 chose a normalized Hadamard matrix over a Householder reflection at the baseline because Householder's entries are balanced only at `N = 4` and its diagonal dominates as `N` grows. That argument presupposes an `N` at which Hadamard's uniform `1/√N` entries are worth roughly nine extra additions per sample. At `N = 8` the fast transform costs `8·log₂8 = 24` additions; at `N = 16`, 64. The decision is coherent at 8.

Second, **the coloration evidence points down, not up.** Dal Santo, Prawda, Schlecht and Välimäki report that spectral coloration grows more noticeable as the number of delay lines falls; ADR-002 cited this as direct evidence that the cost floor is also the quality floor and therefore declined `N = 4` as a baseline. Nothing here changes that, and the density arithmetic below points the same way.

Third, **the cost difference is real but small in the abstract, and is not a CPU claim.** Counting only the core loop — `N` one-multiply damping recursions, `N` per-line gains, the fast Hadamard transform's `N log₂N` additions, and ADR-003's `2N` branchless denormal selects, with injection and output taps excluded because ADR-002 defers them — gives 32 scalar operations per sample at `N = 4`, **72 at `N = 8`**, and 160 at `N = 16`: 1.54, 3.46 and 7.68 Mops/s at 48 kHz. These are *counts*, not cycles. They ignore memory behaviour, which for a 123.5 KiB delay buffer is likely to dominate, and they are not a mobile CPU budget, which remains unmeasured per roadmap.md's LATER table. They are recorded so the bracket's cost ratio (`N = 16` is ≈ 2.2× the baseline; `N = 4` is ≈ 0.44×) is a stated quantity rather than an impression.

#### Why prime lengths, and exactly what co-primality buys

ADR-002 requires mutual co-primality; primes deliver it without a search. Smith's *Physical Audio Signal Processing* states the requirement and its purpose directly — "the delay line lengths in an FDN may be chosen to be mutually prime," which "maximizes the number of samples that the lossless reverberator prototype must be run before the impulse response repeats" — and the same page warns that insufficient mode density "can be heard as 'ringing tones' or an uneven amplitude modulation in the late reverberation impulse response." For the decided set the product of the eight primes is ≈ `6.4 × 10²⁶` samples, about `1.3 × 10²²` seconds; periodicity is not a failure mode this fixture can exhibit.

A second, separable property is worth stating because it is the one that matters inside the audio band, **and worth fencing because it is easy to overstate**. Two delay lines of lengths `mᵢ`, `mⱼ` have comb resonances at `k·f_s/mᵢ` and `l·f_s/mⱼ`; these coincide only when `k/mᵢ = l/mⱼ`, which for `gcd(mᵢ,mⱼ) = 1` forces `mᵢ | k`, i.e. only at integer multiples of `f_s`. Within the band, co-prime lines share no resonance. **The fence:** the coupled FDN's poles are the roots of `det(I − ΓA·D(z))` and are *not* the union of the individual lines' comb frequencies, so this is an argument about coincident echo arrival times and about the uncoupled picture, not an exact statement about the network's pole set. Co-primality removes a known way to get coincident modes; it does not prove the realized poles are well spread. That distinction is exactly the kind NS-6 measures and this ADR does not pre-empt.

Primality is preferred over merely-co-prime composites for three practical reasons: pairwise co-primality is automatic, so the derivation needs no tuple search that could fail or become rate-dependent; the rule stays total and deterministic at any `f_s`; and the `prepare`-time cost is trivial — trial division to `√m ≤ 63` for `m ≤ 3952`, over a handful of candidates per line, off the render thread. Prime *powers* (Smith's alternative) would give finer granularity of achievable lengths, but achievable values are sparse and irregularly spaced, and the nearest-prime rule already lands within 0.43 % of the intended time across all six computed configurations.

#### Why geometric spacing, and why the endpoints are 27 ms and 81 ms

**Geometric, because mode coincidence is governed by length *ratios*, not differences.** Equal ratios between neighbours spread the pairwise ratios as evenly as a fixed range allows; arithmetic spacing compresses the ratios at the long end, so the longest lines become near-duplicates of each other — a drift toward the single-comb behaviour ADR-002 rejected alternative (b) for. The quantitative check that matters for robustness: the adjacent ratio at `N = 8` is `3^(1/7) ≈ 1.17`, a 17 % step, while the prime snap perturbs any single time by at most 0.43 %. The intended spread survives the snap by a factor of roughly forty, and survives a rate change for the same reason.

**The endpoints are set by four constraints that happen to agree.**

1. **Consistency with ADR-002's own arithmetic.** ADR-002 argued for `N = 8` from "eight lines averaging 50 ms, `Σmᵢ ≈ 19200` samples … about 0.4 modes per Hz." The decided set gives `Σmᵢ = 19132` at 48 kHz and a mean of 49.82 ms — within 0.4 % of the figure ADR-002 reasoned from. The fixture therefore *instantiates* the arithmetic that motivated the baseline rather than substituting a different one, which is what makes S2's results comparable to ADR-002's stated expectation.
2. **A physical sanity cross-check, offered as one and not as a design driver.** Smith's treatment regards each delay line as a mean-free-path delay and sets the average delay to Sabine's mean free path `d̄ = 4V/S`. At `c = 343 m/s` the 49.82 ms mean corresponds to `d̄ ≈ 17.1 m` — a large hall. This is consistent with §13's "Large Space" and §45's "extremely spacious," and it is **not** a claim that the fixture will sound like a large hall; ADR-002 already recorded that indistinguishability from a real room is a different goal from Aetherfield's.
3. **The short end belongs to a stage this ADR does not decide.** §33's V0 chain is pre-delay → diffusion → late network. A late network whose shortest line were very short would be generating the early-reflection region that the deferred pre-delay and input-diffusion stages own. Placing `t_min` at 27 ms keeps the late network in the late field. It also keeps the shortest line's comb spacing at `f_s/m_min ≈ 37 Hz`, against 12.3 Hz for the longest line.
4. **`m_min` sets the decay ceiling, and larger is safer.** ADR-004 derives `T60_max` as the largest `T60₀` satisfying ADR-003's margin rule `1 − γ₀^{m_min} ≥ δ_margin`. With `m_min = 1297` at 48 kHz, the closed form `T60₀ ≤ 3·m_min / (f_s · (−log₁₀(1 − δ_margin)))` permits `T60₀` up to ≈ **186.6 s** at the minimum permitted `δ_margin = 1e-3`. The point of that number is *negative*: the margin rule is nowhere near being the binding constraint on a usable decay range, so whoever fixes `T60_max` under ADR-004 will be making a product judgment, not reading off a stability limit. **This ADR does not fix `δ_margin` or `T60_max`;** it makes ADR-004's already-decided formula evaluable and records what it evaluates to at its loosest setting.

**The ratio of 3 is the middle of a real trade.** Toward 1, the lines converge and the network tends to a single comb. Toward large ratios, the longest line sets both the memory ceiling and the time before every line has contributed, while the shortest line's mode spacing widens in the region where there are already fewest modes. A ratio of 3 keeps the adjacent step well clear of the snap error while keeping the longest line at 81 ms.

**One property comes free from ADR-002's "time-domain, not sample count" requirement and is worth making explicit, because it is the reason that requirement was right.** Modal density in modes per Hz is `Σmᵢ / f_s`, which for a time-domain specification is just `Σtᵢ` in seconds. The decided set gives 398.58 ms of total delay at 48 kHz and 398.91 ms at 44.1 kHz — a 0.08 % difference, entirely from prime snapping. **Modal density is therefore rate-invariant by construction**, not by a coefficient that has to be maintained across a rate change. The same holds for the mean free path and the bracket comparison.

#### What the density arithmetic actually says — including a correction to ADR-002's comparison

ADR-002 compared the fixture's ≈ 0.4 modes per Hz against "Schroeder's 0.15 floor" and called it comfortable. That comparison is correct **as Schroeder and Logan stated it, for a one-second reverberation time**, and incomplete as a general statement. The criterion Smith records is `M ≥ 0.15 · T60 · f_s`, i.e. the required mode *density* scales linearly with the decay time — equivalently, it is a constant-modal-overlap criterion, since modal bandwidth is proportional to `1/T60`. Carrying that factor through is only possible once the delay set is concrete, which is why it lands here.

For the decided set, `Σtᵢ = 0.3986 s`, so the Schroeder–Logan criterion is met **up to `T60₀ ≈ 2.66 s`** at either fixture rate. Above that the fixture is below the density that criterion associates with a smooth room-like response: modal overlap falls from 0.88 at `T60₀ = 1 s` to 0.33 at 2.66 s, 0.22 at 4 s, 0.09 at 10 s and 0.03 at 30 s. `N = 4` meets it to 1.35 s; `N = 16` to 5.28 s.

Four things follow, and it matters that they are stated together.

1. **This does not change ADR-002's decision, and does not contradict it.** The criterion scales identically for every `N`, so the ordering `4 < 8 < 16` is unchanged and `N = 8` remains preferred over `N = 4` for exactly ADR-002's reason. What changes is the honesty of the headline: "comfortably above the floor" is true at 1 s and false at 10 s, and a reader of ADR-002 alone could carry the first impression into a long-decay design.
2. **It is not a hard requirement, and must not be treated as a stability result.** Schroeder and Logan's criterion is for indistinguishability from a real room — a goal ADR-002 explicitly disclaimed, recording those figures as "a floor, not a target." Nothing about being below it is unstable, non-finite, or outside any bound ADR-003 proved. The predicted consequence is perceptual: Smith's own phrasing for insufficient density is "ringing tones" or uneven amplitude modulation, which is precisely the coloration ADR-002 named as the open risk of this topology.
3. **The honest conclusion is that this is now a measurable quantity with three named levers, not a solved problem.** A later, evidence-backed ADR can raise density by increasing `N` (the bracket already runs at 16), by increasing `Σtᵢ` (longer mean line, at the cost of a sparser onset and more memory), or by leaving mode density alone and raising *echo* density with the deferred input and output diffusion stages — which is what those stages are for, and which is why §33's V0 chain contains them. Meeting the criterion literally at `T60₀ = 30 s` would demand `Σtᵢ = 4.5 s`, i.e. an architecture far outside ADR-002's `{4, 8, 16}` bracket; that is a decision this ADR has no authority to take and no evidence to support.
4. **Echo density is the axis where `N = 8` is strong, and it is a different quantity.** Counting distinct paths through the network — idealized, ignoring coincidences and any losses — the cumulative arrival count by `k` circulations is at most `(8^(k+1) − 8)/7`: about 8 arrivals by 50 ms, 72 by 100 ms, 584 by 149 ms and 4680 by 199 ms, crossing Schroeder's ≈ 1000 echoes-per-second figure between 100 and 150 ms with **no input diffusion at all**. That is an upper count on an idealized combinatorial model, not a measurement and not a perceptual claim.

#### What the S2 fixture is predicted *not* to show

Stated in advance so that a disappointing S2 audition is not misread as evidence against the topology. The fixture has no pre-delay, no input or output diffusion, no stereo strategy and no output tap design — ADR-002 excludes all of them. Its first wet arrival is therefore at `t_min = 27 ms`, and only eight discrete arrivals occur before 81 ms. **A sparse, audibly discrete onset is a structural certainty of this fixture, by construction, and is not information about the late network.** ADR-002 already fixed the rule: "Sonic quality is explicitly **not** claimed at S2 … Any listening note taken at S2 is an observation, not an acceptance." That stands unchanged.

### Evidence

**No Aetherfield measurement supports this decision. The repository has still measured nothing about reverb (testing.md), and that remains true after this ADR.** The computations below were performed while drafting it, from the decided rule and the stated assumptions, and are reproducible from the rule alone; they are arithmetic, not observations of Aetherfield code.

- **The delay sets, their primality, mutual co-primality and ordering.** For `N ∈ {4, 8, 16}` and `f_s ∈ {44100, 48000}` — six configurations — the rule in (b) produced strictly increasing sets of distinct primes with `gcd(mᵢ, mⱼ) = 1` for every pair, in every configuration. The totality guard (`mᵢ > mᵢ₋₁`) never fired: the unconstrained nearest prime already satisfied it everywhere. The largest displacement of any realized time from its target was **0.43 %** (at `N = 8`, 44.1 kHz, target 31.588 ms → 1399 samples = 31.72 ms); at 48 kHz the largest single displacement was 6 samples (0.13 ms). The largest prime gap below 5000 is 34, which is what makes the `±64` capacity headroom in (b) sufficient.
- **Modal density, and the `T60`-scaled Schroeder–Logan comparison.** `Σmᵢ/f_s` = 0.2030 / 0.3986 / 0.7915 modes per Hz at `N` = 4 / 8 / 16 (48 kHz), and 0.2032 / 0.3989 / 0.7913 at 44.1 kHz — rate-invariant to within 0.1 %, from the time-domain specification. Against `M ≥ 0.15·T60·f_s`, the criterion is met up to `T60₀` = 1.35 / 2.66 / 5.28 s respectively. Modal overlap for `N = 8`, taking modal bandwidth as `2.2/T60`: 0.877 at 1 s, 0.330 at 2.66 s, 0.219 at 4 s, 0.088 at 10 s, 0.029 at 30 s.
- **Margin, decay ceiling and the `a_max` clamp, for the decided set.** With `m_min = 1297` at 48 kHz, `g_max = γ₀^{m_min}` and the margin `1 − g_max` are 0.9544 / 4.56e-2 at `T60₀ = 4 s`, 0.9815 / 1.85e-2 at 10 s, 0.9938 / 6.20e-3 at 30 s and 0.9969 / 3.11e-3 at 60 s — all far above ADR-003's `δ_margin ≥ 1e-3`, whose closed-form ceiling is `T60₀ ≤ 186.6 s`. With `m_max = 3889`, ADR-003's `a_max = 0.999` clamp binds only above `D ≈ 407 dB` at `T60₀ = 0.5 s`, and above ≈ 3 259 dB at 4 s — confirming ADR-003's and ADR-004's statements that the clamp never binds in a plausible damping range, now for the *decided* delay set rather than for illustrative spans.
- **Core-loop operation counts (counts, not cycles, and not a CPU budget).** Per sample, excluding injection and output taps, which ADR-002 defers: 32 ops at `N = 4`, 72 at `N = 8`, 160 at `N = 16`; 1.54 / 3.46 / 7.68 Mops/s at 48 kHz. Reserved delay memory at `C = 3952` floats per line: 61.8 / 123.5 / 247.0 KiB. The idealized cumulative path counts in the Rationale's density-arithmetic subsection follow from `(N^(k+1) − N)/(N − 1)` at a 49.82 ms mean circulation.
- Smith, *Physical Audio Signal Processing* (CCRMA): [Choice of Delay Lengths](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Choice_Delay_Lengths.html) — "The delay line lengths in an FDN may be chosen to be mutually prime. That is, their prime factorizations contain no common factors," which "maximizes the number of samples that the lossless reverberator prototype must be run before the impulse response repeats"; "the delay lengths should be chosen to ensure a sufficiently high mode density in all frequency bands"; and insufficient density "can be heard as 'ringing tones' or an uneven amplitude modulation in the late reverberation impulse response." [Mode Density Requirement](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Mode_Density_Requirement.html) — "the sum of delay-line lengths `Mᵢ` in a lossless FDN is simply the order of the system `M`," which "equals the number of poles on the unit circle in the lossless prototype"; the density is "`M/Fs = MT` modes per Hz"; and, decisively for the Rationale's density-arithmetic subsection, Schroeder and Logan's criterion in its **`T60`-scaled** form, "`M ≥ 0.15 T₆₀ Fs`," stated as "for a reverberation time of 1 second, a mode density of 0.15 modes per Hz is adequate." [Mean Free Path](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Mean_Free_Path.html) — Sabine's `d̄ = 4V/S`, and the practice of "regarding each delay line as a mean-free-path delay" and setting the average delay-line length to it, with the note that this is a lower bound because real reflections are diffuse.
- [Wolfram MathWorld, "Hadamard Matrix"](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/HadamardMatrix.html) — "a necessary condition for a Hadamard matrix to exist is that `n = 1`, `2`, or a positive multiple of 4," and Sylvester's recursive doubling construction. Cited for the claim in (a) that ADR-002's matrix decision, not this ADR, is what restricts `N` to powers of two if the fast transform's `N log₂N` cost is to hold.
- [Dal Santo, Prawda, Schlecht and Välimäki, "Optimizing tiny colorless feedback delay networks," arXiv:2402.11216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11216) — that spectral coloration grows more noticeable as the number of delay lines falls. Already load-bearing in ADR-002; cited again as the reason `N = 4` is a bracket point and not the baseline, and as the named successor method (alternative (b5)) once a measured objective exists.
- [Schroeder, "Natural Sounding Artificial Reverberation," *JAES* 10(3), 1962, pp. 219–223](https://hajim.rochester.edu/ece/sites/zduan/teaching/ece472/reading/Schroeder_1962.pdf) — the ≈ 1000 echoes-per-second and 0.15 modes-per-Hz criteria, already cited in ADR-002 and treated there and here as floors derived for room-indistinguishability, not as Aetherfield targets.
- [Jot and Chaigne, "Digital Delay Networks for Designing Artificial Reverberators," AES 90th Convention, 1991](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=5663) — the homogeneous-decay design whose `gᵢ = γ^mᵢ` makes the delay lengths a free sonic parameter, which is what permits this ADR to choose them on density and spread grounds without disturbing the decay law. Already load-bearing in ADR-002 and ADR-003.

### Consequences

**The S2 verification plan's floor dependency is discharged.** docs/phases/phase1-s2-verification-plan.md's "floor dependency, for every case below without exception" — a decided `N` and a decided `mᵢ` — is now satisfied, and its "at every supported rate" clauses have a concrete two-rate meaning for S2. NS-1's matrix dimension, NS-4's `Γ`, NS-6's per-band realized response, NS-8's `m_max`, and the delay-set floor under every remaining case are all evaluable. **Three quantities still block a subset of cases and are not decided here:** `T60_min` and `T60_max` (NS-5, NS-7, PT-1, PT-2, PT-4, PT-9) and `D_max` (PT-1, PT-2, PT-7). ADR-004 owns the first two as *derivations* that are now evaluable — the closed form and its value at the loosest `δ_margin` are recorded in Rationale — and assigns `D_max` to testing.md's Sonic acceptance gate. Evaluating them is a separate act under ADR-004, not something this ADR performs.

**A delay set is now a versioned quantity, with a migration obligation.** ADR-004 already recorded that "an automation curve or a stored preset is only comparable within a fixed delay set: a later change to `N` or the delay lengths is a state-migration obligation, not a free change." That obligation is now live rather than hypothetical: `T60_min`/`T60_max` derive from `m_min`/`m_max`, so the normalized `[0,1]` Decay value maps to different seconds under a different delay set. Because the product's `N` is still open by design, **S2 results and any presets taken from them must record `N`, `t_min`, `t_max` and `f_s` alongside the numbers**, or they will not be interpretable after a successor ADR changes `N`.

**One of Size's two blockers is removed; Size remains DEFERRED.** ADR-004 recorded that "Size is blocked twice over": its range could not be stated without `N` and the delay-length specification, and a continuously swept Size makes the delay bank time-varying, which ADR-003 (e) Path B bars. The first blocker is now gone — the specification is a pair of times, and a Size control would most naturally be a single multiplicative factor on `t_min` and `t_max`, which the derivation rule already accommodates. **The second blocker stands untouched, and it is the decisive one.** A preparation-time-only Size still cuts the tail, which ADR-004 rejected against §33; a swept Size is delay modulation, which ADR-002 and ADR-003 (e) prohibit. Noting that the rule is *shaped* to accept a scale factor is architectural information, not authorization.

**Reserved capacity, memory and the storage rule.** `C = round(f_s_max · t_max) + 64` is a concrete number (3952 floats per line at 48 kHz; 123.5 KiB at `N = 8`), which makes ADR-003 (d) rule 2 checkable. Its spare capacity is currently unused, because Size and pre-delay are deferred; a later decision on either reopens the rule, since "the longest supported delay time" would then exceed `t_max`. If a rate above 48 kHz is ever supported, the reserve ceiling and this figure scale with it — which is one concrete reason the supported-rate matrix is a decision with consequences, not a formality.

**An honest limitation is now a recorded number instead of an unexamined impression.** The `T60`-scaled Schroeder–Logan criterion is met by the `N = 8` fixture only up to `T60₀ ≈ 2.66 s`, and modal overlap falls to 0.03 at 30 s. This does not violate any bound ADR-003 proved and is not a stability concern; its predicted symptom is the ringing-tone coloration ADR-002 named as the topology's open risk, at exactly the long decay times §13's "Large Space" and "Infinite" regions call for. NS-6 and the Sonic acceptance gate are where this becomes evidence. It is also the strongest argument yet recorded for keeping the deferred diffusion stages on the critical path, since they raise echo density without requiring more modes.

**Nothing above is a sonic result, and no deferral is weakened.** `N_product`, `T60_min`, `T60_max`, `D_max`, the diffusion stages, stereo extraction and tap design, interpolation, the supported-rate matrix, and whether modulation exists at all all remain exactly as ADR-002, ADR-003 and ADR-004 left them. **Modulation in particular is not moved one step closer to authorization by this ADR**; ADR-003 (e)'s Path A and Path B bars stand unmet, and `mᵢ` being concrete makes the delay bank more precisely specified, not more permissible to vary.

### Revisit When

S2's NS/PT results and the Sonic acceptance gate provide the measured evidence ADR-002 deferred the product `N` to, at which point a successor ADR records `N_product` over the `{4, 8, 16}` bracket and this fixture decision is superseded rather than amended; NS-6 or audition identifies the ringing-tone coloration that (e) predicts at long `T60₀`, at which point the three named levers — a larger `N`, a larger `Σtᵢ`, or the deferred diffusion stages — are the candidate responses and the choice must be argued from the measurement rather than from the criterion alone; a measured objective function exists, making alternative (b5)'s optimized delay sets (Dal Santo et al.) a defensible successor to the nearest-prime rule, which today would be optimization without an objective; `T60_max` is fixed under ADR-004 and turns out to be constrained by something other than the margin rule, since the rule alone permits ≈ 186.6 s at `m_min = 1297`; a Size or Pre-delay control is accepted, which reopens both the reserved-capacity rule and the question of whether `t_min`/`t_max` are constants or the endpoints of a scalable range, under ADR-003 (e)'s bars for anything that varies a delay at render time; the product's supported sample-rate matrix is decided — an open gap this ADR deliberately does not close — which fixes `f_s_max`, hence the reserve ceiling and this ADR's memory figures, and which is entangled with ADR-001's deferred AUv3 framework decision; a rate is supported whose ratio to 48 kHz makes the totality guard in (b) fire, at which point the guard's behaviour stops being inert and must be verified rather than assumed; the mobile CPU budget is measured and the `N = 16` bracket point proves unaffordable, or `N = 8` does, which would make the operation counts in Evidence a constraint rather than a note; or ADR-002's matrix-family decision is reopened — for instance by a Path A modulation proposal, which its own "Revisit When" anticipates — since the power-of-two restriction on `N` follows from the Sylvester–Hadamard fast transform and would not survive a different family.
